# 🌿 An Evening Planned with Love — Date Proposal for Sahil

A fully-green, romantic date proposal website featuring a smooth storybook **intro slideshow slip sequence** that glides into a rich, interactive **date night itinerary**.

---

## ✨ Features & Date Itinerary

1. **Slide 1 — The Invitation Intro:**
   * Atmospheric deep forest-green sanctuary with floating starlight/fireflies.
   * Taped polaroid keepsake (`assets/holding.jpg`), heartfelt typography, and a shimmering button to slip forward.

2. **Slide 2 — The Memories & Teaser Slip:**
   * Horizontal page slip revealing favorite memories with Sahil and Maddie (`assets/8.jpg`, `assets/6.jpg`, `assets/10.jpg`).
   * Sneak peek badges teasing the night's upcoming adventures.

3. **Slide 3 — The True Home Page:**
   * **Live Countdown Timer:** Ticking down to **Tonight • 6:00 PM PST**.
   * **Docked Audio Player:** Playing *Notion* by The Rare Occasions with a spinning vinyl disc and live equalizer.
   * **Act 01 (6:00 PM PST):** Minecraft Build Battle Championship 🧱⚔️
   * **Act 02 (7:00 PM PST):** The Grand Dinner Food Roulette for Sahil 🎡🍕 (with an interactive spinning wheel game!)
   * **Act 03 (8:00 PM PST):** Dinner Show: Watch Maddie Play *Five Nights at Freddy's (FNAF)* while Sahil eats 🐻😱
   * **Act 04 (9:00 PM PST):** Collaborative Canva Vision Boarding (dream penthouse, trips, battlestations, pets) 🎨✨
   * **Act 05 (10:00 PM PST):** *WandaVision*, classic Marvel cinematic favorite, or *The Hunger Games* (with an interactive 4K Ultra-HD stream quality verifier) 📺🏹
   * **Act 06 (11:30 PM PST):** Classified Surprise Activities (midnight drive, sweet-tooth drop, keepsake gift & cuddles) 🎁🌙
   * **Interactive Date Preferences Customizer:** Build & save your custom date voucher.
   * **Polaroid Keepsake Wall:** Real couple memories with high-res modal zoom.
   * **Secret Wax-Sealed Letter:** Handcrafted wax seal opening Maddie's personal love letter to Sahil with gold confetti.
   * **The Official Proposal & Playful RSVP:** "Will you be my date?" with a playful dodging "Let me think..." button that runs away, expanding the "YES" button until clicked (triggering a full confetti cannon!).
